A Recipe for Disaster: How Change, Money and Behavior Create Financial Crises

← Back to the Review

Abstract

Financial crises from the South Sea Bubble to the Crash of 1929 have a common set of prerequisites, namely: novel change, easy money, and irrational human behavior. This paper argues that all three must be present to produce a sustained market panic; no single factor is sufficient to cause collapse on its own.
